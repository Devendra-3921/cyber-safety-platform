Financial Fraud Action Plan (India)

Lost Money to an Online Scam or Unauthorized Transaction?

Act quickly. These steps can help. In the event of fraudulent UPI transfers, credit card fraud, or unauthorized bank debits, act as quickly as possible to give authorities the highest chance of freezing fraudulent money trails before withdrawal.

STEP 1: HELPLINE
1930
National Cyber Crime Helpline

Call 1930 and keep these details ready: transaction ID, date and time, amount, and the account or UPI ID involved.

STEP 2: BANK / ISSUER
Block Card / UPI
Card Issuer / Bank Support

Contact your bank or card issuer right away, using the number on your card or the official app, to block the card or UPI.

STEP 3: OFFICIAL PORTAL
cybercrime.gov.in
National Reporting Portal

File a detailed complaint with screenshot evidence, UPI handle, and caller information.

Phishing & Impersonation Messages

Received a Suspicious SMS, WhatsApp, or Email?

Scammers often pretend to be your bank, electricity board, courier service, or a friend asking for urgent help.

What NOT to Do (Red Flags)

  • ❌Don't rush or panic: Urgency is fabricated to bypass your critical thinking.
  • ❌Don't click links or download APKs: Links can lead to fake cloned login forms or spyware.
  • ❌Don't share OTPs, PINs, or card CVVs: No real bank agent or system ever asks for these.
  • ❌Don't send advance processing fees: No legitimate lottery or prize requires paying money first.

What You SHOULD Do

  • ✅Verify via official channels: Open the official app or type the verified URL manually.
  • ✅Call back on a verified number: Look up customer care numbers printed on your physical debit card.
  • ✅Take a screenshot and block the sender: Use your phone's built-in spam reporting feature.
  • ✅Warn family members: Alert elder relatives who might receive the same circulating scam message.
Account Security Recovery

Think Your Account Might Be Compromised?

If you entered credentials on an unknown site or noticed strange login attempts from another device:

Immediate Recovery Steps

  1. Use the official recovery flow: Navigate directly to the provider's real site (e.g., Google Account, WhatsApp Web, or net banking) and trigger official account recovery.
  2. Change your password immediately: Create a strong, unique passphrase not used anywhere else.
  3. Log out of all active sessions: In security settings, select "Sign out of all other devices" to boot unauthorized access.
  4. Enable Two-Step Verification (2FA): Prefer app-based authenticator codes or hardware prompts over plain SMS where possible.
  5. Check linked email & phone numbers: Ensure the attacker did not add their own recovery email or forwarding rules.
