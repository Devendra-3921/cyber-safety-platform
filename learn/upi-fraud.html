Educational Topic #2

UPI & Digital Payment Fraud

Protect your money from fake payment requests and QR code scams.

Overview & Context

Scammers trick you into sending money when you believe you are receiving it. Remember, you never need to scan a QR code or enter your UPI PIN to receive money. Your UPI PIN is for authorising payments, never for receiving money.

Realistic Fictional Scenario
Educational Simulation

Rajesh lists an old bicycle for sale online. A buyer agrees to the price without negotiating and says they will send money immediately.

The "buyer" sends a QR code or a payment request on a UPI app, claiming Rajesh needs to scan it or enter his PIN to "receive" the payment. In reality, it is a request to deduct money from Rajesh's account.

Warning Signs to Watch For

  • Buyers who refuse to meet in person or negotiate
  • Requests to scan a QR code to receive payment
  • Being asked to enter your UPI PIN to accept money
  • Fake payment screenshots showing a successful transfer before it hits your bank

Safe Actions You Should Take

  • You never need to scan a QR code or enter your UPI PIN to receive money.
  • Your UPI PIN is for authorising payments, never for receiving money.
  • Always check your bank SMS or app to confirm receipt of funds
  • Cancel unknown payment requests on your UPI app immediately

The Golden Rule

You never need to scan a QR code or enter your UPI PIN to receive money.
