One-Page Scam Safety Checklist

Keep this handy guide near your workspace or pin it near family devices.

Safe Habits (DO)

  • ✓Verify senders independently using the official banking app or customer care number printed on your card.
  • ✓Use long, unique passphrases for each sensitive account, rather than repeating one simple password.
  • ✓Enable Two-Step Verification (2FA) on WhatsApp, email, social media, and banking portals.
  • ✓Check website URLs carefully before typing credentials (ensure domain spelling is exact).
  • ✓Keep operating systems and banking apps updated to patch known security vulnerabilities.
  • ✓Report suspicious activity immediately to 1930. Quick reporting gives the best chance of stopping the money.

Red Flags (DON'T)

  • ✕Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, or ATM PINs with anyone over phone, SMS, or chat—not even bank staff.
  • ✕Never scan a QR code to receive money. You never need to scan a QR code or enter your UPI PIN to receive money.
  • ✕Don't click unverified links in urgent messages claiming lottery wins, refunds, or electric cut-offs.
  • ✕Don't install screen-sharing apps (AnyDesk, TeamViewer, QuickSupport) on caller instructions.
  • ✕Don't send advance fees for job offers, internship kits, or parcel customs clearance.
  • ✕Don't trust payment screenshots as proof of credit. Always verify your actual bank balance.
